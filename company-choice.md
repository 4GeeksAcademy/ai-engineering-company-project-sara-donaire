# Company Choice

## Company: Nexova Solutions

## Why I chose Nexova

I chose Nexova because AI isn't a side tool there, it's the competitive advantage: CV scoring, candidate matching and first-line support agents are core deliverables, not extras. The problems are very concrete. Each consultant reads 30 to 80 CVs per search by hand, support takes 48 hours to resolve incidents against a 24-hour SLA, and every manager spends 4 to 8 hours a week building a PDF report for Laura. I come from marketing and automation (CRMs, sequences, no-code tools), so I know how these manual workflows look from the inside, and I want to learn how to replace them with real AI systems. Nexova also has five very different teams, which means I'll practise building for different users, data and definitions of success.

## Departments I find most interesting

### Talent Selection Operations (Javier Almeida)
It's the core of the business and the area where AI has the biggest impact. 40 consultants screen CVs manually, there are no email templates, candidates' status isn't tracked in real time (clients call to ask), and matching depends only on intuition. A scoring pipeline plus RAG over the candidate database would change how the whole company works.

### Sales and Business Development
SDRs prospect manually on LinkedIn, export lists to Excel and follow up by email with no automation. Only 40% of the team keeps HubSpot updated, and deals are lost just because follow-up falls through the cracks. This is close to what I've done before, so I'd like to rebuild it properly with automated sequences and alerts for inactive deals.

## The challenge I'm most looking forward to

The AI-assisted selection pipeline with automatic CV scoring and ranking, with explainable rankings. It's a real AI Engineering problem: the ranking has to be useful for consultants and also transparent, so they can trust it and explain it to clients.

## My AI Agent Idea

**CV Screening Agent for Talent Selection**

- **What it does:** When a new search is opened, it reads every CV received, scores each candidate against the vacancy requirements, ranks them and writes a short explanation for each score (for example: "5 years in B2B sales, C1 English, no experience in fintech").
- **What information it needs:** The vacancy briefing from the client (role, must-have and nice-to-have skills, languages, location, salary range), the CVs from the applicant tracking system, and the history of past placements to learn what a good match looked like.
- **What it produces or triggers:** A ranked shortlist with explanations for the consultant to review, a status update for each candidate in the candidate portal, and an automatic email to candidates who move forward or are discarded, using approved templates. The consultant always makes the final decision.
