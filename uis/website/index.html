How a search works

  1. Step 1

    Briefing

    We agree the role, must-have skills, languages, location and salary range with you.

  2. Step 2

    Screening

    A consultant reviews every application against your requirements.

  3. Step 3

    Shortlist and interviews

    You meet a short list of candidates with notes on why each one fits.

  4. Step 4

    Placement

    We close the offer and follow up on onboarding of the new hire.
